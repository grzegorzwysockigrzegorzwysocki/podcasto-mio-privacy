Podcasto Mio Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Podcasto Mio handles information when you use the Android application. Podcasto Mio is published by Gregorio Visocchi Labs ("we", "us" or "our"). For privacy questions or requests, contact GregorioVisocchiLabs@gmail.com.

1. Privacy at a glance

  • You can use the core podcast features without creating an account.
  • Your subscriptions, playlists, playback history, searches, settings, reviews, notes and downloaded audio are stored locally by default.
  • We do not include advertising, third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in this release.
  • Internet requests are required to search podcast catalogues, retrieve RSS feeds, stream or download audio, and use optional Google features.
  • Google sign-in and Google Drive backup are optional and initiated by you.

2. Information handled by the app

The app may process the following information on your device:

  • subscriptions, favorites, folders, tags, playlists and playback queue;
  • playback position, listening history, bookmarks, ratings, reviews and notes;
  • search history, recommendation preferences and app settings;
  • downloaded podcast audio, partial downloads and related metadata;
  • backup and export files you choose to create;
  • content-report details and an optional contact email that you enter;
  • network type and locally counted cellular download usage, used to enforce your download preferences.

If you choose Google sign-in, the app may receive your Google account identifier, email address, display name and profile information made available by Google. Authentication tokens are used to maintain the session and access only the features you authorize; they are not included in personal-data exports.

3. How information is used

Information is used only to provide features you request, including podcast discovery and playback; downloads; your library, recommendations and history; backup, restore and synchronization; data export; network and storage preferences; and support or privacy requests.

4. External services and network disclosures

Podcasto Mio sends requests directly from your device to services needed for the requested feature. Those services receive normal network information such as your IP address, user-agent information and request time, and may receive the search term, language or country used for a catalogue request.

  • Podcast catalogues: Apple iTunes Search, Listen Notes, Podcast Index and Taddy may be queried, depending on which providers are configured and available.
  • Podcast publishers and hosting providers: RSS feeds, artwork, transcripts and audio files are requested from the publisher or its hosting/CDN provider.
  • Google: Google Identity Services are used only if you choose Google sign-in. Google Drive is used only if you choose cloud backup or restore.
  • Android system services: Android may create an encrypted device/app backup according to your device and Google account backup settings.

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising.

5. Google Drive backup

If you enable Google Drive backup, Podcasto Mio requests the limited https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. Backups are stored in your Google Drive application-data area, which is private to the app and is not a general-purpose Drive file permission. A backup may contain the categories you select, such as subscriptions, queue, playlists, history, likes, ratings and settings. Downloaded audio and authentication tokens are not included. You can delete cloud backups using the app's backup controls.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

  • Local app data remains until you delete it in the app, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to any Android system backup you have enabled.
  • Downloaded audio remains until you delete it or an enabled automatic retention rule removes it.
  • Exported files remain wherever you chose to save or share them and must be deleted there by you.
  • Google Drive app-data backups remain until you delete them or remove the app's data/access through Google.
  • Signing out removes the local authenticated session. Account deletion removes the account/session handled by the configured provider but does not automatically delete offline subscriptions, history, playlists or downloads; the app explains this before confirmation.

The app provides controls to clear search and listening history, remove downloads and backups, export personal data, and delete an optional account. You may also contact us to request access, correction or deletion of any information that we control.

7. Permissions

The Android app uses internet access for the network functions described above and a media-playback foreground service to keep audio playing and show playback controls. File selection and export use Android's system picker. Notification permission may be requested for playback and optional new-episode notifications, depending on your Android version and settings.

8. Security

We use HTTPS for configured service endpoints, limit Google Drive access to app data, keep authentication secrets out of exports, and use platform-secure storage where available. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting exported files and access to your device and Google account.

9. Children

Podcasto Mio is a general-audience podcast client and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Podcast content is supplied by third parties; the parental filter can hide content marked explicit but cannot guarantee classification of every external feed.

10. International processing and your rights

External providers and podcast hosts may process network data in countries other than yours. Depending on your location, privacy law may provide rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated, and material changes will be communicated through the app or release information where appropriate.
